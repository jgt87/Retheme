How to use Retheme

Put existing slide content onto a corporate template in four steps. Everything runs on your computer.

  1. 1

    Extract a brandkit

    • Open the Extract brandkit tab.
    • Drop a PowerPoint template (.pptx or .potx) on the upload area, or choose a file.
    • When extraction finishes, review the detected colors, layouts and assets. Correct any color role or asset type that looks wrong, then Save corrections.
    • Optionally Download package as a zip to reuse the brandkit later.
  2. 2

    Retheme a deck

    • Open the Retheme deck tab.
    • Drop your content deck (.pptx) on the Content deck area.
    • Pick the brandkit source: the one you just extracted, an uploaded package zip, or a folder on this computer.
    • Click Retheme deck.
  3. 3

    Confirm your choices

    • Where the deck and the brandkit differ, Retheme asks how to handle it: recolor a color family to the brand color or keep it, replace a foreign font or keep it, choose a layout for unmatched slides, convert headings, and set title sizes.
    • The live preview updates with every selection, so you see the result before committing. Color options show a swatch; font options show the font.
    • Click Apply with these choices.
  4. 4

    Preview and download

    • The Preview page shows the branded deck. Open Logs at the bottom for any notes: retained content, slide-size differences, or an outdated-brandkit warning.
    • Click Download PPTX for a native PowerPoint file built on the template's own layouts, fully editable. Download HTML is also available.
Everything runs on this computer. Your files are processed locally and are never uploaded or sent to any outside service, and no AI is involved.
